# Your reviews on your website — how it works

**For clients. No technical background assumed.**

You have reviews on your Google listing. This puts them on your own website,
keeps them up to date automatically, and does it without slowing your site down
or sending your visitors anywhere else.

---

## What you get

A section on your site showing your real reviews — the reviewer's name, their
star rating, what they wrote, roughly when, and your reply if you left one.

It updates by itself, on a schedule. Nobody has to copy anything across.

## What it costs to run

Nothing per month. There is no widget subscription and no per-view fee. That is
deliberate: the usual way to do this is a paid third-party widget, and we did
not want your reviews to depend on somebody else's billing.

---

## The three things that make this different

### 1. Your visitors' browsers never contact Google

Most review widgets load in your visitor's browser and phone home to a third
party. Yours does not. The reviews are already part of your site by the time
anyone visits.

That means: no third-party tracking attached to your reviews, nothing extra for
your cookie banner to declare, and one fewer company that knows who reads your
site.

### 2. Reviews never silently disappear

If Google changes how its pages work — which happens — our system stops
collecting rather than collecting badly. **Your site keeps showing the reviews
it already had.**

So the worst case is that your reviews are a few days out of date for a while.
The worst case is _not_ that they vanish, or that half of them do. We consider
losing reviews far more damaging than showing slightly old ones, and the whole
system is built around that.

### 3. Nothing is filtered or edited

We show every review we collect. We do not hide low ratings, we do not reorder
to put the good ones first, and we never change a word of what somebody wrote.

If you want to display only four- and five-star reviews, we will tell you we
think that is a mistake and we will ask you to record the reason — but it is
your listing and your decision.

---

## What we need from you

**One thing, in writing: permission.**

We need you to confirm, in an email or on your order form, that you own or
represent the business the listing belongs to, and that you are instructing us
to display those reviews on your site.

This is not a formality we can skip if you are in a hurry. Our system refuses
to collect from any listing without that record on file — it is checked
automatically, and there is no override. That is deliberate, and it protects
you as much as us: collecting reviews for a business that has not asked you to
is a very different activity from doing it at the owner's instruction.

We will also give you a short privacy notice to publish. Reviewer names and
photographs are personal data, and the people who wrote them need a way to ask
for removal.

---

## Honest limitations

We would rather you hear these from us now than discover them later.

|                                                         |                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Updates are not instant**                             | A new review typically appears within a day. Not within minutes.                                                                       |
| **Dates are approximate**                               | Google shows "3 months ago", not a date. We convert that, and we are honest about the precision rather than inventing a day.           |
| **We may not have every review**                        | Google does not always show its own full count. Where we know there is a gap, we record it rather than pretending the set is complete. |
| **Removal is permanent**                                | If a reviewer asks to be removed from your site, that is irreversible on our side. We cannot un-remove them later.                     |
| **Removing from your site is not removing from Google** | Only the reviewer or Google can do that. We can only control what your own site shows.                                                 |

---

## If something looks wrong

Tell us. In particular:

- a review on your Google listing that is not on your site after a couple of days
- a review on your site that is no longer on Google
- anything that looks like somebody else's business

The first is usually a collection pause and will resolve. The second and third
we want to hear about immediately.

---

## Switching to Google's official API

There is an official Google route that requires you to grant us access through
your Google Business Profile account. It is more robust and we can move you to
it at any time — the change takes about an hour and your existing reviews carry
across unchanged.

The trade-off is that Google's official interface does not expose everything
its public pages do; most noticeably, one version of it does not include your
replies to reviews.

We will offer you this when you sign up and record your answer either way. If
you would prefer it now, ask.
