# Code of Conduct

## The Standard

Be straightforward and be kind. Those are not in tension.

This is a small project with a large share of its implementation performed by AI
agents under human supervision. That makes clear, unhedged, technically honest
communication more valuable here than in most places, and it makes personal
friction more expensive.

## Expected Behaviour

- **Review the change, not the person.** "This drops the absence asymmetry" is a
  review. "You clearly didn't read the spec" is not.
- **Be specific.** Name the file, the line, the requirement identifier. Vague
  disapproval costs the recipient an hour and teaches them nothing.
- **Say when you are unsure.** A stated uncertainty is useful information. A
  confident guess presented as fact is the single most expensive thing anyone
  can contribute to this repository.
- **Report your own mistakes early and plainly.** No blame attaches to a defect
  found and reported. Blame attaches to one that was noticed and left.
- **Assume the other person read the same document and reached a different
  conclusion**, rather than that they didn't read it.
- **Disagree with the decision, then support it once it is made** — or escalate
  it through an ADR or EDR, which is what those mechanisms exist for.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery; unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, review, or process.
- Deliberately submitting code you believe to be defective, or approving a
  review you have not read.

## Scope

This applies in every space this project uses — issues, pull requests, commit
messages, code comments, and any private channel where project work happens —
and to anyone acting as a representative of the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub, or through
the private security channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if you would rather
not use a public identity.

Reports are read by a human, kept confidential, and answered. Responses are
proportionate and escalate: a private word, a formal warning, a temporary
exclusion from participation, then a permanent one. The maintainers decide, and
will explain the reasoning to the person affected.

Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is itself a violation and
is treated as a serious one.
